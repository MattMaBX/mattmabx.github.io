To leverage Large Language Models(LLMs) for high-performance Frame-Semantic Parsing, this paper proposes a knowledge-guided Frame-Semantic Parsing method for LLMs. The proposed approach first retrieves relevant knowledge and examples from a frame-semantic knowledge base and constructs knowledge-augmented natural language prompts. It then introduces an example-based constraint optimization method to optimize the prompts. These knowledge-enhanced prompts not only enable the LLM to understand Frame-Semantics but also constrain its outputs. Experiments on the Llama3-8B-Instruct and DeepSeek-v3 models using the FN1.7 and CFN2.0 datasets demonstrate that the proposed method achieves superior performance compared to existing approaches.
